Application for membership

Enrolment is open to eligible members of the Mudaliar community as a Life Member or Annual Member. The management committee keeps the office open for verification and enrolment without needing prior appointment.

What to bring

  • TC or caste certificate
  • Aadhaar card copy
  • Two passport‑size photographs
  • Details of father/husband's name, occupation, and residence & office address

New Life Membership is currently recorded at ₹1,050 per member.
