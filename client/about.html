About the Sangam

Ninety‑eight years of standing with the Mudaliar community.

Regd. No. C‑201/1936 · Karnataka Co‑operative Societies Act


Mysore Vellala Association was established in 1927 and registered under No. C‑201/1936. What began as a small community body has grown into a three‑storey association hall on Manasara Road, Ittigegud, that today houses our office, tenant shops, and community hall — and a membership of roughly 1,450 life members, a 200% increase since 2022.
