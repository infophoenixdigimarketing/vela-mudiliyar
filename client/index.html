About the Sangam

Ninety‑eight years of standing with the Mudaliar community.

Mysore Vellala Association was established in 1927 and registered under No. C‑201/1936. What began as a small community body has grown into a three‑storey association hall on Manasara Road, Ittigegud, that today houses our office, tenant shops, and community hall — and a membership of roughly 1,450 life members, a 200% increase since 2022.

We hold our Annual General Body Meeting every December, run matrimony and job‑placement services for the community over WhatsApp, felicitate merit students and sportspersons each year, and support families through a death‑relief fund. In 2024–25 the Association also amended its original 1936 by‑laws to bring governance in line with the Cooperative Societies Act, extending the elected body's term from one year to three.

01

By‑law amendment & governance reform

The original 1936 by‑laws were amended to match present‑day administrative needs. The elected body's term moved from 1 year to 3 years, with renewal formalities completed under the Cooperative Societies Act.

02

Strengthening our financial sources

All tenant rent agreements were renewed for the next 11 months, rental values revised per agreement and regulation, and documentation updated to cooperative regulatory guidelines.

03

Financial strength & funds

Rs. 18.4 lakhs is currently maintained and renewed as Fixed Deposits in the Association's name, earmarked for future growth and infrastructure.

04

Membership growth & transparency

The Association now has approximately 1,450 Life Members — a 200% increase over 2022 — enrolled through an open, transparent process with basic identity documentation.

05

Office operations & service hours

Timings were extended to Morning 10:00 am–1:00 pm and Evening 6:00 pm–8:30 pm, so members have a dependable place in Mysore for their queries.

06

Reward & recognition for students and achievers

32 students across SSLC, PUC, UG and PG were felicitated with a total of ₹1,01,500 in cash awards; 8 sportspersons and athletes received ₹22,000 under the newly introduced sports award category.

07

Support to needy students, with Savithri Convent

A 50% discount on annual fees was extended to 10 students this year, in continued partnership with Savithri Convent Management.

08

Condolences — departed life members

13 Life Members passed away this year. A death‑fund contribution of ₹80,000 was extended to their families; the families of Sri D.T. Govindaraj Mudaliar, Sri M.J. Aprameya and Smt. C. Maheshwari voluntarily returned the fund for the Association's future welfare work.

09

Matrimony services

Bride and groom profiles continue to be shared through official WhatsApp groups, helping families within the community find alliances.

10

Placement services

Daily job opportunities are shared via the official WhatsApp group, benefiting members, their families and the wider community.

11

Community hall — improvements & future vision

The existing hall continues to serve as a venue for community events. Preliminary analysis, estimation and soil testing are complete for a new, larger hall behind the premises, targeted for our 2027 centenary — funding is being sought through government grants and community donors.

12

Membership cards

Over 90% of members have received their new ID cards; the next batch will be ready by the AGM. Members yet to collect theirs should do so, as this ID will be the only valid proof for future activities, elections and services.

13

Membership scrutiny & electoral transparency

Over 100 members with incomplete or unverifiable information were identified. The General Body is asked to approve restricting such members from future electoral processes, to keep elections fair and legally sound.

14

Annual 2026 calendar

The hanging calendar for 2026 will be distributed to all Life Members, continuing a long‑standing Association tradition.

Scrutiny & finalisation of the Life Membership list

Over 100 Life Members listed by a previous Management Committee have no supporting documents or records in the Association's files, though their names were submitted to the Registrar's office as approved members. In compliance with Sections 16, 18A, 20 and 28 of the Karnataka Co‑operative Societies Act, 1959, the Management Committee proposes:

  • A final 15‑day notice, from the date of the 98th AGM, for these members to visit the office with valid proof of Life Membership.
  • After the cut‑off, unverified memberships will be declared void so the Association can finalise an accurate, valid, updated Life Members list.

If this may concern you or a family member, please bring proof of membership to the office as soon as possible — see contact details below.

Rewards & Recognition, 2024–25

Honouring toppers, and now, athletes too

Every AGM, the Association felicitates Mudaliar community students who excelled in SSLC, PUC and Degree courses, and — new this year — sportspersons and athletes who represented the community with distinction.

Merit award

  • Overall 85% and above
  • 10th (SSLC), PUC, or Degree/UG regular class
  • Academic year 2024–2025 only
  • Open to Mysuru residents only

Sports & athletics award

  • Tournament conducted by a government/aided institution or reputed organisation
  • At District, State, National or International level
  • Candidate secured 1st, 2nd or 3rd prize

How to nominate

  • Collect the nomination form from the office
  • Submit TC and mark sheet copy, or tournament details & certificate for athletes
  • Submit an Aadhaar copy
  • Anyone may submit the form on the nominee's behalf

All nominations must reach the office no later than 5 December 2025. Last‑minute requests cannot be considered.
