In loving memory

13 Life Members passed away in 2024–25. The Association paid its last respects and extended a death‑fund contribution of ₹80,000 to their families — a gesture some families, including those of Sri D.T. Govindaraj Mudaliar, Sri M.J. Aprameya and Smt. C. Maheshwari, generously returned for the Association's future welfare work.

If your family has lost a Life Member and this hasn't reached us, please let the office know so we can honour them too. See contact details.
