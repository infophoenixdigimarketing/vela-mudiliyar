Rewards & Recognition, 2024–25

Honouring toppers, and now, athletes too

Every AGM, the Association felicitates Mudaliar community students who excelled in SSLC, PUC and Degree courses, and — new this year — sportspersons and athletes who represented the community with distinction.

Merit award

  • Overall 85% and above
  • 10th (SSLC), PUC, or Degree/UG regular class
  • Academic year 2024–2025 only
  • Open to Mysuru residents only

Sports & athletics award

  • Tournament conducted by a government/aided institution or reputed organisation
  • At District, State, National or International level
  • Candidate secured 1st, 2nd or 3rd prize

How to nominate

  • Collect the nomination form from the office
  • Submit TC and mark sheet copy, or tournament details & certificate for athletes
  • Submit an Aadhaar copy
  • Anyone may submit the form on the nominee's behalf

All nominations must reach the office no later than 5 December 2025. Last‑minute requests cannot be considered.
